For this project, I utilized HackerRank and Google Workspace (specifically Google Forms and Google Sheets) to develop both technical and collaborative skills. On HackerRank, I completed the beginner-level 'Solve Me First' challenge, which provided hands-on practice with fundamental syntax and problem-solving logic. For the collaboration component, I designed a 5-question "Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz" using Google Forms, incorporating multiple-choice and short-answer formats to assess digital safety knowledge. I then linked this form to a Google Sheet to automatically organize and view incoming responses.

These platforms offer significant academic benefits. HackerRank serves as an interactive environment to reinforce classroom programming concepts, allowing me to test my coding abilities against real-world logic problems and prepare for technical assessments. Meanwhile, Google Workspace tools are invaluable for academic research and teamwork. Mastering Google Forms enables efficient data collection for surveys and primary research, while Google Sheets provides a structured way to analyze that data. Together, these tools improve both my independent technical proficiency and my ability to collaborate and manage information effectively in group projects.
