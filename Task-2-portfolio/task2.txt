Basically, the days of just handing in a paper resume and hoping for the best are over. Whenever you apply for a job, an internship, or even reach out to a professor for a project, the very first thing they will do is Google your name. What they find (or don't find) makes a massive first impression. If you don't exist online, or if your online presence is messy, it can hurt your chances.

What we are actually doing in this task:
Instead of letting our digital footprint be an accident, this assignment is about taking control of our own story. We are going to build our "digital front door."

This means we will be:

Setting up the basics: Creating clean, professional profiles on the main platforms that recruiters and professors actually check (like LinkedIn, a personal website, or a digital portfolio).

Showcasing our best selves: Making sure that when someone searches for us, they see our skills, our projects, and our professional goals right at the top.

Future-proofing our careers: Getting this done now so that when that dream internship opens up, we are already prepared and looking incredibly professional online.
