Honestly, what surprised me most while researching cybercrime wasn't some crazy, high-tech hacking stuff like you see in movies. It’s how insanely good scammers are at playing with human psychology. I realized that most UPI frauds don't happen because an app has bad security; they happen because scammers create a fake sense of panic or urgency, making you act before you think. It was a huge wake-up call to realize that literally anyone could fall for these tricks on a busy or stressful day. Because of this project, I'm definitely changing my password habits. I’ve been guilty of using the same variation of one password for pretty much everything since high school. Starting today, I’m setting up a password manager and finally turning on two-factor authentication for my email and bank apps so I don't become a cautionary tale.
